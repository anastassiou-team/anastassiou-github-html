Biophysics of Computation

Welcome to the website of the Costas A. Anastassiou lab. We are part of the Department of Neurosurgery at the Cedars-Sinai Medical Center. We use a multidisciplinary approach blending biology, engineering, computer science and statistics to understand how cellular makeup shapes neural activity, in health and disease.

About the Lab

How Do Cell Types Shape Human Cognition?

Despite unprecedented insights into the brain's composition we still do not understand how cellular activity organizes at the circuit level and bridges to cognitive computations. This challenge is particularly acute in humans, where we lack the tools to establish causal links between cell types and brain computations. How do human neurons of all flavors combine to produce cognition? What principles underly such computations? Can these computations be reverse-engineered and simulated from first principles?

We develop tools to unravel causal links in human microcircuit dynamics and cognition in health and disease. To do so, we adopt a multidisciplinary approach combining biophysics, statistical learning, optimization, computational simulation, and biophysics-informed AI.

Projects

Growing Cells in Computers

Building biophysically detailed neuron models constrained by multimodal data sets (patch-clamp electrophysiology, morphological reconstructions, single-cell transcriptomics, etc.). Using evolutionary and gradient-based optimization to generate models across cell types to create digital twins of biological neurons.

Single-Cell Transcriptomics Electrophysiology
Click for details

Foundational model of brain circuits

Bottom-up data-driven models of human neural circuits, integrating multimodal data to understand brain function and dysfunction. Simulating cortical microcircuits with thousands of biophysically detailed neurons, validated against Neuropixels recordings and local field potential data.

Computational Neuroscience Human Brain Multi-modal Data
Click for details

Brain Disease Modeling

Investigating cellular and circuit-level mechanisms underlying neurological diseases including epilepsy and glioma. Using patient-derived human brain tissue and multi-modal characterization to build computational models of seizure dynamics and aberrant neural activity.

Epilepsy Disease Modeling Human Tissue
Click for details

Monitoring and Modulation Technologies

Developing and applying advanced technologies for monitoring and modulating brain activity, including Neuropixels high-density neural probes and deep brain stimulation. Investigating how extracellular electric fields interact with neural circuits at the cellular level.

Stimulation Extracellular Recordings Neuromodulation
Click for details

Biophysics-informed ML & NeuroAI

Developing biophysics-inspired, -informed, and -constrained artificial intelligence. Leveraging spiking neural network architectures and biophysical neuron models to create brain-inspired computing systems and AI models grounded in biological realism.

Machine Learning AI Biophysics
